Create Custom & Innovative Education Systems

Large EdTech corporations routinely develop standardized platforms with little room for flexibility, showing no regard for your organization’s unique pedagogy. As a result, forward-thinking educators face highly restricted choices.

xSoTec has extensive experience building tailored systems as robust or as practical as you need. From Competency Gradebooks and Behaviour Management Systems to Attendance Trackers and Student Data Dashboards, xSoTec allows your organization to build, refine, and implement the creative solutions your organization needs.

Build in Weeks, Not Years

xSoTec leverages Google Workspace and our own proprietary tools to create systems specifically to your organization’s needs. Because we don’t have the limitations of traditional software development, we can collaborate with your school and generate a system specific to your needs in a matter of weeks.

Iterate, Refine, and Test New Ideas

xSoTec employs an iterative-based approach to development, meeting with you frequently to brainstorm and modify your system as you see fit. Thanks to our speed, this approach allows you to test ideas, gather feedback from stakeholders, and implement new solutions rapidly, so you see tangible improvements even after launch. xSoTec empowers educators to never stop improving.

Dashboards for the Entire School Community

No matter what you build, give every stakeholder access to the data they need. xSoTec allows you to build individualized dashboard views for parents and guardians, students, teachers, and administrators.

All Your Disparate Systems, Connected

Generate original data, connect to Google Classroom, or upload from almost any other data source. Your xSoTec system can enhance the data from the systems you already have.
