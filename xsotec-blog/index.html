Social Innovation Award and Updates from xSoTec

  • Post author By xsotec
  • Post date December 15, 2020


We wanted to share the good news! xSoTec founder, Jay Cohen, is honored to have been nominated for “The Greater Philadelphia Innovation Awards”. Please take a moment to vote for xSoTec and the other great innovators from the Philadelphia area that are up for nomination. Then, share this with all your networks! You can find us in #5 – Education Innovation, Jay Cohen, xSoTec. Voting ends this Friday, December 18th so show your support soon. Thank you!

Here is the link to the voting form…

https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/SIAWARDSROUND2

For background…

https://socialinnovationsjournal.org

https://www.socialinnovationspartners.org

If you want to know what we’ve been doing, here it is below. And if you know of any school looking to build their own unique student or family technology system, please let us know!

We’ve built a simple system for connecting hundreds of Google Spreadsheets together to create innovative student and family educational applications. 

The systems we build allow teachers and family members to finally have a say in how students are graded, how behavior is handled, or even how attendance works. Over the past few years, small schools around the country have been trying out our educational applications and developing systems that would blow you away!

We’re currently working with about a dozen or more schools and it would be great to have more. But, just between us, the real goal is to eventually use the system to help families build collaboration systems so that we can better organize and solve our own community issues. We’re hoping that this award will help us do just that. We’ll keep you posted!
